Voice

Direct. Confident. Without ceremony.

We say

  • Trustworthy agents for high-value workflows.
  • Compress tool chains. Aggregate actions.
  • Decisions you can audit.

We don't say

  • Revolutionary. Game-changing. Paradigm-shift.
  • Unlock the power of. Supercharge. Leverage.
  • The future of AI is here.

Verbs over adjectives

If you can't show it doing something, cut it. Adjectives describe; verbs commit.

Specific over sweeping

"Reduces tool-call latency by 60%" beats "blazing fast." Numbers and named systems over hype.

One idea per sentence

If a sentence has two commas and an em-dash, it has two sentences in it. Split them.
