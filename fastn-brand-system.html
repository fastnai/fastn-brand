04 · Voice

Direct. Confident. Without ceremony.

Fastn speaks like an engineer briefing a teammate. Short sentences. Concrete nouns. No marketing fog.

Tone

We say

  • Trustworthy agents for high-value workflows.
  • Compress tool chains. Aggregate actions.
  • Decisions you can audit.
Anti-patterns

We don't say

  • Revolutionary, game-changing, paradigm-shift.
  • Unlock the power of, supercharge, leverage.
  • The future of AI is here.
Rule

Verbs over adjectives

If you can't show it doing something, cut it. Adjectives describe; verbs commit.

Rule

Specific over sweeping

"Reduces tool-call latency by 60%" beats "blazing fast." Numbers and named systems over hype.

Rule

One idea per sentence

If a sentence has two commas and an em-dash, it has two sentences in it. Split them.

Rule

Lower-case in product

Sentence-case for UI strings. Title-case is reserved for the wordmark and proper nouns.

WCAG AA, by construction.

Focus

Visible, always

Interactive elements get a 3px rgba(79,178,255,0.18) ring on focus. Never outline:none without a replacement.

Motion

Respects reduced motion

All transitions honor prefers-reduced-motion. Decorative animation is opt-in, not opt-out.

Targets

44px minimum

Tap targets meet the 44×44px WCAG 2.5.5 target. Density modes shrink visuals, not hit boxes.
